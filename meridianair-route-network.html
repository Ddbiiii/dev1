Passenger desk

Two routes, two different arrivals

The fictional network connects a Jakarta origin with a city break and an island arrival. This is a route illustration, not an operating schedule.

  1. JKT → SIN

    Singapore is the city-break branch of the concept. The sample board uses a direct journey of 2 hours 5 minutes.

  2. JKT → DPS

    Denpasar is the island-arrival branch. The sample board uses a direct journey of 1 hour 50 minutes.

  3. Planning a connection

    A city-pair line is only one part of a trip. Consider terminal changes, baggage handover and onward travel separately.

Connections without loose ends ↗

Treat the transfer as its own journey: arrival gate, onward terminal, baggage and documents each need a clear answer.
