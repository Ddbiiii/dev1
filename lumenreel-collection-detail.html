Slow Weather: a viewing order

The collection follows the way weather changes a person's sense of time. Its titles are fictional, and the order is a curatorial suggestion.

Begin with observation

After the Rain Index establishes a patient rhythm through small details and repeated returns.

Move into memory

A Map of Salt turns landscape into a place where the past interrupts the present.

Leave a little space

The Quiet Signal closes the sequence with a smaller scale and a question that does not need an immediate answer.

How an evening becomes a programme ↗

A programme note should explain the arrangement without deciding the viewer's response. Lumen Reel uses a question, a sequence and a practical viewing frame.
