Nacre / field notes

The crossing is the question

The Cartographers of Nacre is a fictional exploration game about a landscape that remembers more than its maps admit.

Read

Notice pressure, sediment and distant silhouettes before trusting a route drawn by someone else.

Compare

Overlay old maps at the light table. An inconsistency can be a mistake, a changed landscape or an intentional omission.

Choose

Decide which witness account to carry forward. The ledger changes future conversations rather than awarding a universal moral score.

Three instruments, three limits ↗

The instruments help you notice the world, but each leaves something unresolved. That gap is where the player makes a judgement.
